## Summary

V040-010 has two evidence layers:

- Historical mechanics trial: two direct Codex App Handoff operations on June 18, 2026 proved Local to Worktree and Worktree to Local Handoff availability, but did not use a Groundwork `native_handoff_package` path.
- Strict release-gate trial: a second Local to Worktree and Worktree to Local Handoff pair used `artifacts/v0.4.0-codex-native-worktree-handoff/release-gate-native-handoff-package.md` as the native handoff package path on clean release candidate commit `b3e3d2c`.

The strict trial closes the previous package-path gap. It validates local Codex App Handoff mechanics and package-path visibility for this host and release candidate. It does not claim UAT readiness or external marketplace publishing.
